FAQ

Questions? We have answers

How do I pay with UPI?

Choose UPI at checkout to view our boutique QR code. You can scan it directly, tap the one-click payment button to open GPay/PhonePe/Paytm on your phone, or download the QR code image to your gallery.

How long does custom stitching take?

Blouses and kurtis usually take 4–6 days; salwar suits take 5–8 days; bridal lehengas and festive gowns require 12–20 days. You will receive live WhatsApp updates as soon as your garment is ready for trial.

How do I track my order status?

Use the Track Order section below with your Order ID (starts with SD) and 10-digit phone number to see exact milestone stages and view your official receipt.

Can I apply discount coupons?

Yes! During checkout in your cart, enter any active boutique coupon code and click Apply to instantly receive your discount.

Do you offer alterations?

Yes, all custom stitched outfits come with internal margin cloth, and minor fitting adjustments are completely complimentary.
