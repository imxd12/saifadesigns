Privacy Policy & Measurement Protection

At SAIFA DESIGNS Atelier, we treat your bespoke body measurements, personal contact details, and style archives with the utmost care, confidentiality, and respect.

Last updated: October 2026 · Effective for all boutique visitors and atelier patrons.

1. Data Fiduciary Identity & Contact

SAIFA DESIGNS Ladies Tailor & Atelier acts as the Data Fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 for all personal data collected through our boutique website and atelier studio.

Atelier Workshop: Shop No. 12, Gulshan Market, Main Road, New Delhi 110025, India
Official Support: +91 92892 41183 · Grievance Email: privacy@saifadesigns.com

2. Personal Data We Collect & Why

We collect only the specific data necessary to deliver handcrafted couture garments and fulfill bespoke tailoring services:

  • Bespoke Body Measurements: Bust, waist, hips, armhole, sleeve length, shoulder breadth, front/back neck depth, and length preferences. Collected solely to cut individual pattern papers and stitch garments to your body contours.
  • Contact Information: Full name, 10-digit mobile number, and delivery address. Collected solely to coordinate fitting milestones, dispatch couriers, and send live WhatsApp order status updates.
  • Payment Reconciliation Data: UPI reference IDs (UTR numbers) and order amounts. We do not store bank account credentials, PINs, or card CVVs.
  • Order & Fitting History: Garment designs, fabric swatches, and past alterations, stored so repeat orders fit effortlessly without re-measuring.

3. Stringent Non-Disclosure & Security Controls

  • Zero Data Monetization: We will never sell, rent, commercialize, or disclose your contact details or body measurements to third-party telemarketers, brokers, or ad platforms.
  • Cryptographically Masked Lookups: Our online order tracking masks customer names and mobile numbers. Only authenticated boutique staff can access unmasked order and client records.
  • Progressive Rate-Limiting & Anti-Brute-Force: Public lookup endpoints are protected against automated scraping and brute-force enumerations with cryptographic tokens and lockout penalties.

4. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal, you have statutory rights guaranteed under Indian law:

Right to Access:

You may request a complete summary of your measurements and orders archived at our atelier.

Right to Correction:

Update your measurement profile whenever your fit changes before a new garment cutting begins.

Right to Erasure:

Request the complete deletion of your body measurements, contact details, and archived fit profile.

Grievance Redressal:

All privacy requests are addressed within 72 business hours by our dedicated grievance contact.

🛡️ Request Measurement Profile Erasure

Under Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023, you may request the deletion of your saved measurement profile and fit records at any time.
