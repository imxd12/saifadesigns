Alteration Guarantee & Return Policy

We stand proudly behind every stitch. Discover our complimentary 7-day alteration guarantee and exchange guidelines.

Effective date: October 2026 · SAIFA DESIGNS Atelier, New Delhi.
✨ SAIFA ATELIER PROMISE

1. Complimentary 7-Day Fit Alteration Guarantee

Every bespoke outfit tailored at SAIFA DESIGNS—whether a bridal lehenga, silk blouse, or everyday kurti—comes with our Complimentary 7-Day Alteration Guarantee:

  • Free Adjustments: If any area feels slightly snug, loose, or needs hem adjustment, we adjust it completely free of charge within 7 days of collection or delivery.
  • In-Boutique Master Fitting: You may visit our boutique at Shop No. 12, Gulshan Market, New Delhi, where our master craftsman will personally pin and adjust your garment.
  • Fast Turnaround on Alterations: Minor alterations are completed promptly, typically within 24 to 48 hours.

2. Bespoke & Custom Stitching Returns

Because bespoke garments are individually drafted, cut, and hand-stitched to your personal body measurements, custom tailoring cannot be returned for a cash refund once the fabric has been cut.

Instead, our full dedication is invested into perfecting your fit through our complimentary alteration service until you are 100% delighted with the silhouette.

3. Ready-to-Wear (Readymade) Ensembles

  • Exchange Window: Unworn, unwashed readymade garments with all original boutique tags intact may be exchanged for a different size or style within 7 days of purchase.
  • Condition: Garments showing signs of wear, perfume scents, makeup stains, or alterations by third-party tailors are ineligible for exchange.
  • Store Credit: If your desired replacement size is out of stock, we provide 100% boutique store credit valid for 6 months.

4. Cut-Piece Fabrics & Materials

Unstitched fabrics that are cut from wholesale rolls to custom lengths (e.g. 2.5m, 3.5m) cannot be returned once cut, as cut fabrics cannot be reattached. If a fabric is found to possess a manufacturing weave defect prior to cutting, we will immediately replace it or issue a full refund.
