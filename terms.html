Atelier Craftsmanship Agreement

Terms of Service & Client Agreement

Transparent, fair, and artisan-honoring policies guiding your bespoke stitching, fabric purchases, and atelier visits.

Effective date: October 2026 · Governing all transactions at SAIFA DESIGNS, New Delhi.

1. Scope of Boutique & Atelier Services

SAIFA DESIGNS operates a premier boutique and ladies tailoring atelier at Shop No. 12, Gulshan Market, Main Road, New Delhi 110025. Services provided include:

  • Custom pattern drafting and bespoke stitching for ladies' suits, bridal lehengas, festive gowns, designer blouses, and kurtis.
  • Curated sales of pure unstitched fabrics (Chanderi silk, modal satin, pure georgette, organza, mulmul cotton).
  • Ready-to-wear tailored ensembles and boutique accessories.
  • Complimentary alteration adjustments on all garments stitched at our atelier.

2. Measurements & Fit Assurance Guarantee

We believe every woman deserves an impeccable fit. Measurements can be submitted via our online form, recorded in-person at our New Delhi atelier, or copied from a favorite physical sample garment.

  • Complimentary 7-Day Fit Guarantee: Every bespoke garment includes free minor alterations (taking in, letting out within seam allowances, adjusting hem length or sleeve fit) within 7 days of collection or delivery.
  • Seam Allowances: All custom garments are crafted with generous inner margin allowances (1.5 to 2 inches) allowing future ease adjustments as your body naturally changes.
  • Design Fidelity: Handcrafted garments are created individually; subtle hand-embroidery placement nuances are normal characteristics of artisanal craftsmanship.

3. Crafting Timelines & Delivery

Crafting timelines begin once measurements and advance confirmation are finalized:

  • Designer Blouses & Daily Kurtis: 4–6 business days.
  • Salwar Suits & Anarkalis: 5–8 business days.
  • Bridal Lehengas & Heavy Festive Gowns: 12–20 business days.
  • Artisan Rest Day: Our atelier is closed on Mondays to allow our craftspeople to rest. Production days count Tuesday through Sunday.

4. Pricing, Payments & Order Holding

  • UPI & Payment Options: We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) directly to our verified merchant handle, as well as Cash at the boutique.
  • Unclaimed Finished Garments: Finished garments are held safely in our climate-controlled atelier storage for up to 60 days following trial notification.
